# Acabat compartit de tots els biomes

Eina integrada image_gen. Dos atles de tres columnes i dues files, ordre de lectura: Galeries glacials (7), Jardins subterranis (10), Fractures geotèrmiques (13), Abisme biològic (16), El Llindar (19), El Nucli (20). Cada cel·la de terra es converteix en una textura repetible independent en carregar-la; no es repeteix l’atles sencer. Les cel·les dels mòduls només són recursos visuals, no creen noves bases. El Nucli conserva la seva estructura final sense taller nou.

Recursos: `assets/textures/terres_biomes_atlas.png` i `assets/sprites/bases_biomes_atlas.png`.

## Prompt del terra

Production game texture atlas: EXACT 3 columns by 2 rows, six equal SQUARE cells, no gaps, no borders, no labels. Entire canvas landscape aspect ratio 3:2. Each cell is its OWN seamless repeating opaque top-down FLOOR MATERIAL texture, no perspective, no objects rising off ground, no large boulders, no scenery. Consistent fine detailed crisp pixel art stone clusters for SUBTERRA MARS caves, matching a dark crystalline slate cave floor. Tile scale identical across cells, uniform low-contrast lighting. Order row-major: 1 GLACIAL CAVES cold navy gray rock plates with fine pale blue frosted seams and thin ice crust, restrained highlights; 2 SUBTERRANEAN GARDENS dark olive basalt with small muted moss patches and fine root traces, no plants standing up; 3 GEOTHERMAL FRACTURES dark charcoal red basalt plates with small dull ember-orange fissures, no lava rivers, keep mostly dark stone; 4 BIOLOGICAL ABYSS dark plum charcoal rock with subtle violet organic mineral films and sparse dim pink bioluminescent flecks; 5 THE THRESHOLD dark graphite and old bronze mineral sediment, sparse angular ochre geometric veins embedded in natural fractured stone, no floor panels; 6 THE CORE deep charcoal teal rock with fine turquoise mineral seams and tiny amber specks. Each cell must tile seamlessly to itself independently at its four edges. Limited palettes, pixel clusters and irregular cracks throughout. No blurry clouds, no smooth gradient, no text, no icons. Six materials filling their entire cells.

## Prompt dels mòduls

Create a game SPRITE ATLAS of 6 variations of the reference Mars cave base module. EXACT 3 columns and 2 rows, six equal square cells with identical scale, no borders, no text. Landscape canvas 3:2. TRANSPARENT background. Each module uses the reference's SAME silhouette, isometric camera angle, door on front left, cyan window on right, antenna above, support feet directly on ground, no pedestal or surrounding landscape. Fit complete antenna and feet inside each cell, same bottom ground-contact line 90% down each cell, centered horizontally; consistent transparent padding. Detailed crisp pixel art matching reference. Row-major variations: 1 GLACIAL CAVES dark blue-gray metal with pale blue frost on roof edges and cyan service accents; 2 SUBTERRANEAN GARDENS dark olive gunmetal with small moss traces along lower supports and green service trim; 3 GEOTHERMAL FRACTURES dark heat-blackened metal with copper orange trim and soot on lower edges; 4 BIOLOGICAL ABYSS dark plum gunmetal with magenta violet trim and faint organic patina at supports; 5 THE THRESHOLD dark graphite with old bronze trim and a few small angular mineral engravings; 6 THE CORE dark graphite teal with turquoise trim and tiny amber service lights. All have cyan lit window and antenna, small amber hazard stripes, recognizable airlock door. No new buildings or machinery, no rocks around base. Every variant remains a reusable base module in the same game world.
